# Issue and pull-request triage

## Response goals

- acknowledgement within three calendar days;
- substantive response within 14 days; and
- resolution, explicit deferral, or closure target within 90 days.

These are service goals, not guarantees. A short honest queue update is better
than leaving contributors uncertain.

## Label dimensions

Use a small composable vocabulary:

- `type:` bug, feature, data, case, docs, dependencies;
- `area:` api, runtime, schemas, cases, plugins, cli, docs, ci;
- `skill:` python, economics, provenance, data-license, writing;
- `effort:` 30-min, half-day, multi-day, deep-design;
- `status:` needs-triage, needs-repro, ready, claimed, blocked, review; and
- `needs:` domain-review, license-review, security-review.

`good first issue` means the scope, files, acceptance criteria, and review contact
are known. Do not use it as a synonym for low priority.

## Triage outcome

Every triaged item should have an owner or explicit status, bounded acceptance
criteria, relevant skill and effort labels, and a link to any superseding
Discussion or RFC. Close duplicates with a useful pointer. Close declined work
with a reason tied to the charter, roadmap, or maintenance capacity.

Never move vulnerability or sensitive conduct details into a public queue.
